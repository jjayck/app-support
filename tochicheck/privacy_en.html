Privacy Policy

Effective: October 14, 2026 (updated for ads: October 14, 2026)

This policy explains how the iPhone app "Tochi Check" (the "App") handles user information.

Information stored

Land results you check, candidate lots you save and destinations you register (workplace, station, etc.) are stored only on your iPhone. The developer does not receive them. No account is required.

Location

Only when you tap the current-location button, the App uses your iPhone's location to move the map. It is not sent to the developer and is not stored outside your device. However, the advertising provider below may obtain approximate location to deliver ads. You can use the App without allowing location: just move the map and pick a lot.

Network communication

  • Hazard-map tiles of the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism ("Hazard Map Portal Site") and elevation tiles of the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan: map tile numbers around the point you check are requested, which reveals the approximate area to the servers. No name, email address or device identifier is sent.
  • Apple Maps services (MapKit): used for address and place search, address display, travel-time estimates and the "nearby places" list. Handled under Apple's privacy policy.

Servers may log connection information such as IP addresses as a matter of how the internet works. The developer does not obtain or use it.

Advertising

The App shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC) at the bottom of the screen. To deliver ads, Google may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), approximate location, device information and ad interaction data. This is handled under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); the developer does not receive this personal information.

After the first launch, iOS asks whether to allow tracking. If you allow it, ads may be personalized. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the App works the same and ads are not personalized. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

The App uses no analytics tools other than the above.

Disclosure to third parties

Except for the map-data requests, Apple Maps and ad delivery (Google AdMob) described above, the developer does not provide user information to third parties.

Disclaimer

The App summarizes data published by national and local governments. It does not guarantee safety. Before buying land or building, always confirm with your municipality's hazard map and official documents. The App is not affiliated with any government body.

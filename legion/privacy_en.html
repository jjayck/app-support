Privacy Policy

Effective: November 10, 2026 (updated: November 14, 2026) ・ 日本語

This policy explains how the iPhone app "Element Legion" (the "App") handles user information.

1. Information we collect and why

(1) Device information: an anonymous ID created by the App, the IP address of the connection, and the result of Apple's device check (App Attest). Used to identify your game data and to prevent cheating.

(2) Gameplay information: records of Crystals, captains, recruits and rewards. Used to provide the game and to prevent cheating.

(3) Purchase information: Apple's transaction ID, product, date and refund status. Used to deliver purchased items and to handle refunds.

(4) Transfer information: the transfer code and your game progress (kept for 30 days). Used to move your data to a new device.

(5) Display language: whether your device is set to Japanese or another language. Used only to choose the language of messages from our server; it is not stored.

(6) Support requests: the contents of your email. Used to reply to you.

We do not collect your name, address, location, contacts or advertising identifier.

2. External modules

The App does not use any third-party modules for advertising or analytics.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your information with third parties except as required by law. Data is stored on a cloud service operated by a company in the United States.

4. Deleting your data

To request deletion of your data, contact us at the address below with the support ID shown on the "How to Play" screen of the App.

5. Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page.

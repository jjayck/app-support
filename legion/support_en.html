FAQ

How do I play?

Tap the title screen, then tap "Sortie" to start a battle. Touch the screen to bring up the stick and move your squads (turn on AUTO to move automatically). Attacks are automatic. Use the Circle, Charge and Spread buttons to change formation. Defeat the giant beast that appears after 2 minutes to clear the stage.

Does it cost money?

The game is free to play. Crystals, the Starter Pack and the Lantern Pass (monthly) can also be purchased in the App. Prices are shown in the App before you buy.

What happens to my Crystals when I change iPhones?

On your old iPhone, open the "How to Play" screen and issue a transfer code. On your new iPhone, enter that code on the same screen. Your Crystals, captains and progress will move over (the code is valid for 30 days and can be used once). The Lantern Pass can also be restored with "Restore Purchases" in the Crystal shop on the Recruit screen.

How do I cancel the Lantern Pass?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then "Subscriptions". The pass renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

The game is running slowly.

Close other apps, then restart the App.

FAQ

What can I play for free?

Chapter 1 of the Ash Corridor and its boss, the Light-Eating Bishop, all three endings, Daily Depths with leaderboards, and The Depths down to B10 are free (Daily Depths and The Depths open after you defeat the Bishop). The one-time purchase "Lantern Legacy" unlocks Chapters 2-8, The Depths from B11 onward and the Twisted Castle, and gives you revive and blessing rewards without watching ads. There are no random-draw purchases and no subscriptions.

"Lantern Legacy" is gone after changing or reinstalling

Tap "Restore Purchases" in the shop. With the same Apple ID you used to buy it, you get it back at no charge. Restoring requires an internet connection.

Can I move my save data to another iPhone?

Progress is saved only on your iPhone. The App has no account or data transfer feature. Restoring from an iPhone backup may carry it over, but this is not guaranteed.

When do ads appear?

Only when you choose one yourself: "Watch ad to revive", "Ad: resume from B..." or "Ad: one more blessing". Ads never appear on their own. Watch to the end to receive the reward; if no ad is available, you receive the reward anyway. With "Lantern Legacy", you get the same rewards without ads.

Can I play offline?

Playing, progressing and claiming rewards work offline. Only buying "Lantern Legacy" and "Restore Purchases" need an internet connection (if you see "Waiting for a connection", check Airplane Mode and your signal). The date for Daily Depths is synced with the server time whenever a connection is available.

My score doesn't appear on the Game Center leaderboard

Make sure you are signed in under Settings > Game Center. Scores are sent each time you clear a floor.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 16 or later. The game is played in landscape.

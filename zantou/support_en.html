FAQ

What can I play for free?

All eight chapters of the Ash Corridor and its boss, the Light-Eating Bishop, all three endings, Daily Depths with leaderboards, and The Depths down to B10 are free (Daily Depths and The Depths open after you defeat the Bishop). The one-time purchase "Lantern Legacy" unlocks The Depths from B11 onward and the Twisted Castle, and gives you revive and blessing rewards without watching ads.

What can I buy?

One-time purchases "Lantern Legacy", "Starter Pack" and "Growth Pack", plus crystal packs, Moon Passes and the Pilgrim's Pass. Crystals (used at the gear altar and elsewhere) are also earned by playing. The gear altar gives random gear, and the odds are shown on the altar screen. Moon Passes are not subscriptions and do not renew: for 30 days you receive the daily amount when you open the App that day (days you do not open it are not carried over). There is no stamina system. Legal notices are shown from the shop.

My purchases are gone after changing or reinstalling

"Lantern Legacy", "Starter Pack" and "Growth Pack" come back with "Restore Purchases" in the shop, at no charge, with the same Apple ID. Restoring requires an internet connection. Crystals, Moon Passes and the Pilgrim's Pass (consumables) are recorded only on the device and cannot be restored after changing devices or deleting the App.

Can I move my save data to another iPhone?

Progress is saved only on your iPhone. The App has no account or data transfer feature. Restoring from an iPhone backup may carry it over, but this is not guaranteed.

When do ads appear?

Only when you choose one yourself: "Watch ad to revive", "Ad: resume from B..." or "Ad: one more blessing". Ads never appear on their own. Watch to the end to receive the reward; if no ad is available, you receive the reward anyway. With "Lantern Legacy", you get the same rewards without ads.

Can I play offline?

Playing, progressing and claiming rewards work offline. Only purchases and "Restore Purchases" need an internet connection (if you see "Waiting for a connection", check Airplane Mode and your signal). The date for Daily Depths is synced with the server time whenever a connection is available.

My score doesn't appear on the Game Center leaderboard

Make sure you are signed in under Settings > Game Center. Scores are sent each time you clear a floor.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 16 or later. The game is played in landscape.

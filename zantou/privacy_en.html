Privacy Policy

Effective: October 22, 2026 ・ Updated: November 5, 2026 ・ 日本語

This policy explains how the iPhone app "Zantou: Lantern Depths" (the "App") handles user information.

Information stored

Your game progress (chapters and stars, deepest floor reached, talents, gear, endings, conversations in town, whether you finished the tutorial) and the record of your in-app purchases are stored only on your iPhone, in the App's own storage. The developer does not receive this information. There is no account.

To investigate bugs, the App may keep a technical log on the device (frame rate, results of purchase processing, etc.). This log is never sent off the device.

Stored data is deleted when you delete the App. Depending on your settings, it may be included in your iPhone backups.

Time check

To count days correctly for Daily Depths and daily tasks, the App connects to a static website published by the developer (GitHub Pages, jjayck.github.io) when it starts or returns to the foreground, and reads only the server's time. No name, device identifier or game data is sent.

As with any internet connection, the host (GitHub) may record connection information such as your IP address. The developer does not obtain or use it.

In-app purchase

Purchasing and restoring in-app purchases is handled by Apple's App Store. The developer does not receive any payment information. Purchases are verified on the device using Apple's StoreKit.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the App submits the deepest floor you reach in Daily Depths and The Depths to Game Center leaderboards. Your Game Center nickname and score are visible to other players. Game Center is covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can play without signing in.

Advertising

The App does not show ads. It uses no advertising service, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not track you (since November 5, 2026).

Disclosure to third parties

Except for the time check, and the App Store and Game Center described above, the developer does not provide user information to third parties.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 22, 2026 ・ 日本語

This policy explains how the iPhone app "Zantou: Lantern Depths" (the "App") handles user information.

Information stored

Your game progress (chapters and stars, deepest floor reached, talents, gear, endings, conversations in town, whether you finished the tutorial) and the record of your "Lantern Legacy" purchase are stored only on your iPhone, in the App's own storage. The developer does not receive this information. There is no account.

To investigate bugs, the App may keep a technical log on the device (frame rate, results of ad and purchase processing, etc.). This log is never sent off the device.

Stored data is deleted when you delete the App. Depending on your settings, it may be included in your iPhone backups.

Time check

To count days correctly for Daily Depths and daily tasks, the App connects to a static website published by the developer (GitHub Pages, jjayck.github.io) when it starts or returns to the foreground, and reads only the server's time. No name, device identifier or game data is sent.

As with any internet connection, the host (GitHub) may record connection information such as your IP address. The developer does not obtain or use it.

In-app purchase

Purchasing and restoring "Lantern Legacy" is handled by Apple's App Store. The developer does not receive any payment information. Purchases are verified on the device using Apple's StoreKit.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the App submits the deepest floor you reach in Daily Depths and The Depths to Game Center leaderboards. Your Game Center nickname and score are visible to other players. Game Center is covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can play without signing in.

Advertising

The App shows rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. An ad appears only when you choose it yourself: revive, resume from a checkpoint ("Insurance"), or an extra blessing. If you own "Lantern Legacy", you receive the same rewards without watching ads, and no ads are shown.

To deliver ads, Google may handle your device's advertising identifier, device information, ad view and interaction data, and approximate region estimated from your IP address. This is handled under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); the developer does not receive it.

On first launch, iOS asks whether to allow tracking. The advertising identifier is used for tracking (personalized ads) only if you allow it. If you decline, the App works exactly the same. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Disclosure to third parties

Except for ad delivery, the time check, and the App Store and Game Center described above, the developer does not provide user information to third parties.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 11, 2026 (updated: October 21, 2026) ・ 日本語

This policy explains how the iPhone app "Mawashite Shindan" (the "App") handles user information.

Information stored

The App requires no account and the developer runs no server. Player names and answers you enter during a quiz are handled only inside the App to run that quiz and are never sent outside your device. The developer does not receive them.

Quiz results are not saved on the device. They disappear when you close the App.

Sharing results

Some quizzes let you share a result image. The image is sent only when you tap the share button, through the iOS share sheet, to a destination you choose.

Advertising

The App shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC): a banner at the bottom of the screen and full-screen ads, for example after a result screen. To deliver ads, Google may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), device information and ad interaction data. This is handled under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); the developer does not receive this personal information.

On first launch, iOS asks whether to allow tracking. If you allow it, the advertising identifier may be used to personalize ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the App works the same and ads are still shown, but not personalized. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

The App uses no analytics tools other than the above.

Disclosure to third parties

Except for ad delivery (Google AdMob) described above, the developer does not provide user information to third parties.

Disclaimer

The quizzes in the App are for entertainment only. Results are not scientific, medical or psychological assessments. The developer is not liable for any damage arising from the use of the results.

FAQ

Which quizzes are included?
Five: "Zombie Movie" (who dies first), "Oshi Type" (16 fan types), "Mirror" (how others see you), "Two of Us" (for couples) and "User Manual" (your own instruction sheet). Pass one iPhone around and everyone answers in turn.

Are results saved?
No. Results disappear when you close the App. To keep one, use the share button where available, or take a screenshot.

How do I show a result to others?
"Zombie Movie" has a share button on the result screen that sends an image. For the other quizzes, show the screen directly or take a screenshot.

Are the results accurate?
The quizzes are for entertainment only. They are not scientific or medical assessments.

Can I remove the ads?
There is currently no option to hide ads.
